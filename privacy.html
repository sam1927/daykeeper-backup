Privacy policy

Daykeeper backup. Last updated 06/11/2026.

Who uses it

Daykeeper backup is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

It asks Google for the drive.file permission only. That means it can see and change only the files it has created itself in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see any other files, emails, contacts or account details.

What it stores

Encrypted copies of the owner's journal backups, in one folder in the owner's own Google Drive. The files and their names are encrypted on the owner's home server before upload. Google, and anyone else without the owner's key, sees only scrambled data.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or sent anywhere other than the owner's own Google Drive. There are no adverts, analytics or tracking.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections and delete the backup folder from Google Drive.
